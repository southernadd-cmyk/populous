# Original Populous influences and current direction

The original *Populous* links level land to settlement, settlement to population, and population to more power over the land. Its high-level influence over autonomous followers is the central reference for this game. *Populous II* broadens the terrain powers. *Populous: The Beginning* provides a useful 3D sense of the shaman, followers, landmarks, and spells. The bundled [original game manual](populous.pdf) remains a reference for terrain and settlement behaviour.

The Living Faith uses that land-and-followers loop for a **non-combat** contest. A level 2×2 patch creates room for a hut; level neighbours and births grow it through a house, fort and castle, with larger foundations claimed by the building. Builders act on available plots, while the player picks where to shape, where to send the shaman, and whether each blessed site should Grow, Worship or Guard. Owned stones have visible spiritual health and can change hands under shaman pressure. Faith buys actions; productive sites earn Devotion.

The 64×48 world spaces the starting villages across a central crossing and offers five sacred sites on upper, lower and middle routes. A minimap keeps the territory navigable. Terrain and foliage use instanced meshes, while land shaping updates only the changed tile's terrain slots. This retains the single-tile land decision as the world grows.

Future design work should be driven by human playtests. Candidate improvements are clearer path previews on difficult terrain, stronger visual feedback as villages expand, terrain-specific choices around each stone, and a responsive rival without perfect information. Avoid adding seasons, combat, factories, or extra currencies to solve a pacing issue.
