How to play

Welcome to the alpha build. Features, balance and UI are still being tested, so expect rough edges. Choose a topic below; the world pauses while this guide is open.

Win the faith race

Reach 4,000 Devotion and complete a festival in each region: North, Crossing and South. Both conditions are required. Ember follows the same rules.

Faith is what you spend to reshape the world. Followers, developed homes and stone circles replenish it over time.

Devotion is your score. Mature settlements, worship and stone circles generate it. The first festival in each region adds 900 bonus Devotion.

Your role is environmental rather than managerial. You do not tell braves what job to do or which settlement strategy to follow. Change the land and they react.

  1. Shape settlement landCreate clear, level ground. Settlers automatically claim useful 2×2 plots and braves gather timber to build.
  2. Shape the economyPlant groves to create timber and hunting grounds; expose mineral seams to draw miners and speed later development.
  3. Shape sacred groundPrepare stone foundations and terraces. Developed sacred landscapes attract worshippers and eventually trigger festivals automatically.

Watch what the population does, then alter the environment to encourage different behaviour.

Settlements emerge from the land

A new hut needs a clear, level 2×2 plot near your existing settlement or an owned stone circle. You do not place huts directly. When useful ground exists and the tribe needs room, settlers claim it automatically.

Raise Land and Lower Land each change one tile by one height level for 4 Faith. Height 0 is water; land ranges from 1 to 5. Heights 1–3 support woodland. Heights 4–5 can expose underlying geology, but only some tiles contain mineral-bearing rock. Those geological veins are fixed when the world is generated. Crossing below height 4 closes any exposed seam; raising mineral-bearing ground back into the highlands makes it prospectable again.

Home upgrades

Homes use the village-wide workforce. They grow automatically when land, household growth and age requirements are met. Hunting provisions speed population growth; mined stone shortens the wait toward forts and castles.

Because settlements are autonomous, where you create flat land matters. Good terrain invites expansion; trees and mineral deposits around it change what spare workers are likely to do.

Influence people through the world

Braves choose their own work. They build available projects, gather timber, tend belief, worship developed sacred places, defend threatened stones, hunt, mine and explore.

Groves · height 1–3Plant Grove creates a small cluster rather than one tree. Each tree stores finite timber for construction. Cutting can thin a grove, but surviving woodland lets harvested trees slowly return and young trees mature again. Dense woodland also pulls spare workers toward hunting.

Mineral geology · height 4–5Look for subtle metallic traces on high ground or use Inspect to find mineral-bearing rock. Expose Minerals reveals the fixed deposit beneath that tile. Richer geology creates a larger seam, attracts more mining activity and lasts longer before exhaustion.

Open landDistant clear country encourages scouting. If woodland or minerals dominate locally, fewer spare workers scout; large open surroundings make exploration comparatively attractive.

There are no Grow, Worship or Guard settings. Sacred terraces themselves attract worshippers; a threatened stone attracts a keeper automatically. If you want different behaviour, alter the landscape that makes that behaviour useful.

Shape sacred ground

Each glowing rune starts as empty sacred ground. Make a clear, level 2×2 foundation containing the rune. Bring your shaman within three tiles, choose Build Stone, and click a valid foundation. Nearby braves construct the circle.

A completed circle produces Faith and Devotion. A rival shaman standing close drains its spiritual health. Your own nearby shaman, worshippers and an automatically attracted keeper can resist that pressure.

The eight tiles around the rune form its terrace. Matching four to the centre height strengthens the site and begins attracting worship. Matching seven prepares that region for a festival and attracts the worshippers needed to celebrate.

There is no Guard button or protective Ritual. Defence comes from the shape of the sacred landscape, the followers it attracts and where you move your shaman.

Festivals emerge automatically

You do not press a Festival button. Prepare the environment and the tribe celebrates when the conditions are met.

  • The site must belong to you and have at least 55 belief.
  • A stone needs a seven-tile level terrace, or a nearby home must reach house in North/South or fort at the Crossing.
  • The prepared site automatically attracts two worshippers; they must arrive before the festival can begin.
  • The site must be healthy and your tribe must have at least 70 Faith. The celebration then happens automatically and spends that Faith.

The first festival in each region permanently marks it complete and awards 900 bonus Devotion plus the normal festival reward. The tribe then has a 60-second festival cooldown.

Every spell changes the environment

Your shaman must be within 5.5 tiles of the target for these environmental powers. Invalid targets do not spend Faith.

Plant Grove 12 Faith

Create a small grove across at least three nearby clear height 1–3 tiles. Each tree holds a finite timber reserve. Builders gradually thin woodland; harvested trees can regrow when enough neighbouring trees survive. Raising woodland into high ground removes it.

Expose Minerals 16 Faith

Reveal the deposit beneath clear height 4–5 mineral-bearing rock. Barren high ground cannot produce ore. Subtle surface traces and Inspect reveal prospectable geology before you spend Faith. Rich geology creates a larger finite seam.

Raise Land 4 Faith

Raise one tile by one level, up to height 5. Use it to create settlement plots, terraces, routes and higher ground.

Lower Land 4 Faith

Lower one tile by one level, down to water at height 0. Use it to level useful ground or cut routes.

Land Bridge 30 Faith

Turn water along a short line into low land, changing movement routes across rivers and lakes.

Commands and camera

The only non-environmental controls are movement and inspection. Persistent player changes happen on the map.

Move Shaman Free

Click reachable land to send your shaman there. Shaman position matters around sacred sites and determines where environmental powers can be used.

Build Stone 20 Faith

Construct a permanent map feature on a prepared sacred 2×2 foundation. Braves finish the structure automatically.

Inspect Free

Read homes, followers, resources and sacred sites. Inspect never changes their behaviour directly.
