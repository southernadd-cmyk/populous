How to play

You are the Verdant. Your rival, the Ember, expands on the far side of the island. Only one settlement begins on each side. Raise the marked 3×3 patch near yours to found another settlement in a few seconds. Growth occurs every few seconds, and your followers generate faith for miracles.

Raise / Lower: move a 3×3 patch up or down one of six land heights. A new village needs a flat, fertile patch 5–13 tiles from an existing one. Towns expand onto nearby tiles of the same height. Bloom: make land fertile and attract followers. Revelation: inspire people near your influence, including rival worshippers. Blight: weaken a rival settlement's food supply. Quake: ruin buildings and farms; repair damaged land by sculpting or Bloom. Rally beacon: send followers on a free pilgrimage to exert steady religious pressure.

Select Inspect worshipper and click a person to watch their needs and chosen action. Followers use hunger, fatigue, safety, devotion and local needs to choose tasks. Click a settlement card to jump to it and watch changing allegiance. Win by converting Ashfall and sustaining 65% of all worshippers for 20 ticks. Bloom your villages to ward off a foretold Ember attack; Revelation is less effective when repeatedly cast on the same village.
