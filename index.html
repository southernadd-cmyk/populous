Grow a living faith

Shape a clear, level 2×2 plot near a village or an owned sacred stone. When land opens, followers plan new huts, gather wood and build them. As a family grows, level more clear ground around its hut: four neighbouring tiles, three births and 25 seconds make a house; six neighbours, six births and 85 seconds make a fort on a clear 2×2 footprint; all eight neighbours, ten births and 160 seconds make a castle on a clear 3×3 footprint. Each stage also needs time to mature before the next. The buildings grow automatically and add housing, Faith and Devotion when blessed. Inspect a home to see its next milestone; hover while shaping to preview an upgrade. Sacred sites begin as glowing runes. Flatten a 2×2 foundation containing the rune, bring your shaman within three tiles, choose Build Stone and click the top-left tile. Nearby followers build the circle automatically.

Bless a completed hut or finish a stone circle to start earning Devotion. Faith is the resource you spend; productive sites earn Devotion towards 1200. Inspect your site to choose Grow (builders, belief and nearby settlement), Worship (two worshippers and faster Devotion), or Guard at a stone (a caretaker and worshipper protect spiritual health). Workers weigh nearby construction, low belief and local staffing before choosing jobs. They stay near villages when support is needed. Worship and Guard still reserve the followers you assign. A festival gives an immediate Devotion reward when belief reaches 55, two worshippers have arrived and you have 70 Faith. A festival puts celebrations on a 60-second cooldown across your tribe.

Raising and lowering each change one tile for 4 Faith. Four aligned tiles around a stone give its full terrace bonus. A rival shaman slows construction and can drain a finished circle’s coloured spiritual health; move your shaman near it, choose Guard, or perform a protective Ritual. Bridges open routes over water. Every world has mountain ridges, hills, two rivers and lakes. Click the minimap to jump across the world. Drag to move the map with your pointer, right drag to rotate, and scroll to zoom. There is no combat.
