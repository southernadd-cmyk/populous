How to play

You are the Verdant. Your rival, the Ember, expands on the far side of the island. Only one settlement begins on each side. Raise the marked 3×3 patch near yours to found another settlement in a few seconds. Growth occurs every few seconds, and your followers generate faith for miracles.

Raise / Lower: move a 3×3 patch up or down one of six land heights. A new village needs a flat, fertile patch 5–13 tiles from an existing one. Towns expand onto nearby tiles of the same height. Bloom: make land fertile and attract followers. Revelation: inspire people near your influence, including rival worshippers. Blight: weaken a rival settlement's food supply.

Select Inspect worshipper and click a person to watch their needs and chosen action. Followers use hunger, fatigue, safety, devotion and local needs to choose tasks. Watch the settlement cards for changing allegiance. Win by sustaining 70% of all worshippers for 30 seconds.
