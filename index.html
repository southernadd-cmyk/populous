How to play

You are the Verdant. Your rival, the Ember, expands on the far side of the island. Settlements grow on level fertile land and generate faith, which fuels divine actions.

Raise / Lower: sculpt land to create room for growth. Bloom: make land fertile and attract followers. Revelation: inspire people near your influence, including rival worshippers. Blight: weaken a rival settlement's food supply.

Followers use needs, danger, faith and opportunity to choose their own actions. Watch the settlement cards for changing allegiance. Win by sustaining 70% of all worshippers for 30 seconds.
