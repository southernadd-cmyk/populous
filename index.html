Shape the story

Your followers build and worship on their own. You choose where their world grows.

One world. Three regions.

Earn 4,000 Devotion and hold a festival in the North, Crossing and South before Ember does. The runes mark each region. A growing village, a stone circle, or a mix can carry your faith there.

  1. Shape landMake room for homes and level sacred terraces.
  2. Guide followersBless homes; choose Grow, Worship or Guard at a site.
  3. Hold festivalsPrepare a site in each region and celebrate.

Level ground brings growth

Raise or Lower changes one tile for 4 Faith. A clear, level 2×2 plot near your settlement or owned stone draws a new hut. Followers gather wood and finish it. Hover with a land spell to preview useful tiles.

HUTHOUSEFORTCASTLE

A family needs clear level neighbours, family growth and time to advance: a house needs 4 tiles / 3 growth / 25s; a fort needs 6 / 6 / 85s and a clear 2×2 footprint; a castle needs 8 / 10 / 160s and a clear 3×3 footprint. Each stage also matures before the next. Inspect a home for its progress.

Use Land Bridge to open routes over the rivers and lakes.

A tribe with its own plans

Braves choose nearby building, tending and village support according to need. They gather wood, build open plots and help families grow. Convert a grey wildman near your shaman for 20 Faith when you have room for them.

Bless a finished home for 30 Faith to produce Devotion. Inspect a blessed home or stone circle to set its policy:

GROW keeps workers available for building and local belief.

WORSHIP assigns two followers for faster Faith and Devotion.

GUARD gives a stone one caretaker and one worshipper against a rival shaman.

Build a faith work

Faith pays for actions; productive blessed homes and completed circles earn Devotion. To build at a glowing rune, level a 2×2 foundation containing it, bring your shaman within three tiles, then choose Build Stone and click the plot’s top-left tile. The circle costs 20 Faith; followers finish it.

For a regional festival, a home within 5½ tiles of a rune must be a house in North or South, or a fort at Crossing. A stone needs seven of its eight terrace tiles level with its centre. The site also needs 55 belief, two present worshippers, healthy support and 70 Faith. Festivals share a 60-second cooldown.

A rival shaman can drain an owned circle’s coloured spirit bar. Bring your shaman, set Guard or use Ritual to protect it. There is no combat.
