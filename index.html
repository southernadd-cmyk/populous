How to play

Choose a topic below. The world pauses while this guide is open.

Win the faith race

Reach 4,000 Devotion and complete a festival in each region: North, Crossing and South. Both conditions are required. Ember follows the same rules; the first tribe to complete both wins.

Faith is what you spend. Followers produce a little over time; blessed homes, worshippers and built stone circles add more. You start with 45 Faith and can store up to 160.

Devotion is your score. Finished stone circles and blessed homes earn it over time. The first festival in each region adds 900 bonus Devotion on top of its normal award.

The five glowing runes mark sacred sites: two in the North, one at the Crossing and two in the South. Complete a region by celebrating at a prepared home near one of its runes or at a stone circle built on that rune. You can expand villages, develop stone circles, or combine both.

  1. Grow a villageShape a level plot, let braves build a hut, then bless the finished home.
  2. Prepare a festival siteBuild a stone circle on a rune or grow a blessed home near one. Assign two worshippers and keep the site healthy.
  3. Celebrate across the mapHold a festival in North, Crossing and South while your sites earn 4,000 Devotion.

Watch the top bar for Faith, Devotion and regional progress. Your shaman travels to shape land and cast spells; braves gather wood, build, support homes and worship.

Make room for homes

A new hut needs a clear, level 2×2 plot near one of your completed buildings or owned stone circles. Trees, water, other structures and sacred runes block a plot. Choose Plan Hut and click its top-left tile for free. Settlers can also plan huts on available plots, especially ones you have just shaped. Braves gather wood and build; a planned hut does not earn Devotion until it is finished and blessed.

Raise Land and Lower Land each change one tile by one height level for 4 Faith. Height 0 is water; land ranges from 1 to 5. Match the surrounding height to open plots and improve existing homes. Hover with a land spell to preview a new plot or building upgrade. You cannot reshape an occupied tile or a built stone foundation.

Home upgrades

Nearby working braves help a household grow. A home upgrades automatically when its land, household growth and age all meet the next row. The age is measured from when the hut was completed. The fort and castle also need time since the previous upgrade.

Guide, then let them work

Your shaman moves where you send them and casts spells within range. Braves make their own decisions: they gather wood and build projects, tend low-belief sites, or stay near homes and stones to support them. Their choice depends on distance and what needs attention. Grey wildmen can join your tribe with Convert if you have housing.

Use Inspect on a blessed home or your completed stone circle to set a policy. A policy assigns people automatically; you do not need to click individual braves.

Grow releases assigned worshippers to work. Nearby level plots can attract new huts; working braves support the site’s belief and household growth.

Worship assigns up to two braves to the site. They must arrive before their extra Faith and Devotion count, and two present worshippers are required for a festival. Fewer workers remain available to build.

Guard is available at your stone circles. It assigns one keeper and one worshipper. The keeper helps resist a visiting rival shaman, but one worshipper is not enough for a festival.

The game keeps at least three braves free for work when assigning worshippers or a keeper. If a site cannot fill its policy yet, it will assign people as they become available.

Build and protect a circle

Each glowing rune starts as empty sacred ground. Make a clear, level 2×2 foundation containing the rune. There are four possible positions for that square. Move your shaman within three tiles of the rune, choose Build Stone, and click the foundation’s top-left tile. It costs 20 Faith. Braves travel there and build the circle. A rival shaman nearby slows their work.

A completed circle produces Faith and Devotion. Its coloured bar then shows spiritual health, rather than building progress. A rival shaman standing close can drain that health. If it reaches zero while the rival is beside the circle, the circle changes owner. Empty runes cannot be claimed with Ritual; they must first be built.

Your nearby shaman, local worship and a Guard keeper help the circle recover or resist capture. A protective Ritual restores health and briefly shields it. The circle’s colour shows its owner: green for you, orange for Ember. If you lose one, you can weaken the rival’s built circle with Ritual and bring your shaman beside it to convert it.

The eight tiles around the rune form its terrace. Matching four to the centre height gives its full Faith and Devotion production bonus. Matching seven prepares it for its first regional festival. Inspect a terrace tile to see its current alignment.

Complete each region

A festival is held through the Inspect panel of your blessed home or finished stone circle. To mark an uncompleted region, use either a circle with seven level terrace tiles, or a blessed home within 5.5 tiles of that region’s rune. A North or South home must be at least a house; a Crossing home must be at least a fort.

When the Festival button becomes available

  • The site belongs to you and has at least 55 belief.
  • Two assigned worshippers have reached the site. Set Worship and allow them time to travel.
  • The site is healthy: a completed friendly hut and two working braves are nearby. A stone can also stay healthy without a hut if two worshippers are present and at least four terrace tiles match.
  • You have 70 Faith, and your tribe’s 60-second festival cooldown has ended.

The first festival in a region permanently marks it complete and awards 900 bonus Devotion plus its normal festival award. Later festivals still award Devotion but do not count for another region. A festival lowers that site’s belief by 18; belief recovers when the site is healthy. At a stone it also restores 12 spiritual health. Completing a region remains credited even if you later lose its site.

Belief and spiritual health are different: belief shows how well a site is supported and affects its income and festivals; spiritual health is the ownership bar on a stone circle. Inspect the site to see its belief, worshippers, terrace and cooldown.

Spend Faith where it matters

Select a spell in the bottom bar, then click its target. Your shaman must be within 5.5 tiles of the clicked tile for Convert, Bless, Raise, Lower and Land Bridge. Ritual instead needs the shaman within three tiles of a completed circle. Invalid targets do not spend Faith.

Convert 20 Faith

Click a grey wildman. They become a brave in your tribe if you have room under your housing limit.

Bless Hut 30 Faith

Click a finished, unblessed home. A blessing makes it earn Devotion and add Faith income; it stays blessed as it grows.

Ritual 30 / 45 Faith

On your circle, spend 30 Faith to restore 35 spiritual health and shield it for 12 seconds. On Ember’s completed circle, spend 45 to remove 35 health. Bring your shaman right beside it: reducing health to zero while close converts it. Ritual cannot claim an empty rune.

Raise Land 4 Faith

Raise one tile by one level, up to height 5. Raising water makes land; matching heights can open hut plots and strengthen terraces.

Lower Land 4 Faith

Lower one tile by one level, down to water at height 0. Use it to level plots or terraces; lowering land to water cuts a route.

Land Bridge 30 Faith

Click water to turn water tiles along a line of up to five tiles into low land. Join two shores to give your shaman and braves a route across.

Commands and camera

Choose a command in the bottom bar, then click the map. Commands stay selected until you choose another. The small hint below the bar describes the selected action.

Move Shaman Free

Click reachable land to send your shaman there. They walk around water when a land route exists.

Plan Hut Free

Click the top-left tile of a valid, clear 2×2 plot near your settlement or owned circle. Your shaman does not need to stand beside it; braves build it.

Build Stone 20 Faith

Click the top-left tile of a level 2×2 foundation containing an empty rune, with your shaman within three tiles of the rune. Braves finish the circle.

Inspect Free

Click a friendly blessed home or sacred site to view progress and set its policy or hold a festival. Click other objects or land to identify them.
