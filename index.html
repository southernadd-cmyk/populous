Guide your tribe

This is a continuous real-time contest inspired by Populous: The Beginning. Your shaman converts wildmen and casts spells near her. Braves gather wood, complete hut plans and defend the settlement. Occupied huts produce more braves; every follower helps replenish mana.

Convert a wildman, place huts near your camp, then use Attack to send braves at the red tribe. Raise land and Land Bridge change the paths your followers can use. Blast damages enemies and buildings. Enemy braves build and raid while you play. Hold your shaman near the central shrine for three seconds to break the enemy hearth ward and increase mana income. Attack sends at most three braves with an eight-second cooldown. Destroy the enemy hearth and defeat its shaman to win; defend your hearth to survive.

Click Move Shaman and a destination to bring her within casting range. Pause or change speed at any time. Right drag or use ↶ ↷ to rotate; scroll to zoom. This is a first playable foundation, not a recreation of all units, spells or networking.
