Grow a living faith

Build a village, welcome wildmen, and guide your shaman to three sacred stones. Followers provide a small base income. Assign workers to an owned sacred stone or blessed hut to produce much more faith. Worshippers cannot gather wood or build until recalled. Each worship site has local belief. Keep a completed hut and at least two working braves nearby to raise it and improve worship income; neglected sites lose belief. Inspect a site to see its belief. Your total faith earned becomes devotion; the first tribe to 400 devotion wins.

Spend faith to convert wildmen, bless a completed hut for permanent income, or shape land. Hold your shaman beside a sacred stone for three seconds to claim it. A Ritual claims a nearby stone immediately for 45 faith, or protects one you already own for 25 seconds at a cost of 30 faith. The Ember tribe builds, assigns worshippers, and claims stones too.

Click Move Shaman and a destination. Drag to pan, right drag to rotate, and scroll to zoom. Pause or adjust the speed at any time. There is no combat.
